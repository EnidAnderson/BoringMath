Method: each tag was checked against the actual literature online.

`https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/XXXX`; book tables of contents were
read from the books' own front matter (d-nb.info / pageplace previews /

matches the cited use), **VERIFIED-CORR** (the pin exists but the repo's
assignment/section needs a one-line correction — corrected pin given),
**VERIFIED (section level)** (chapter/section title confirmed from the EGA
contents listing; proposition-level numbers not re-verified page-by-page),
**PARTIAL** (bibliographic pin confirmed, cited content lives elsewhere),
**STILL-UNPINNED** (checked, but not verifiable online — what was checked is
recorded).
