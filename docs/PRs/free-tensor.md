# Candidate PR: the constructive tensor of basis-carrying free modules (`pr/formal-periods-free-tensor`)

Ticket: P.1.2 (#107), leaf of P.1 (#93). Depends on P.1.1 (#106, landed:
`BoringMath/FormalPeriods/FreeMod.lean`) and the P.0 lint
(`BoringMath/FormalPeriods/AxiomLint.lean`).

## Statement

One new module `BoringMath/FormalPeriods/FreeTensor.lean` (plus this note):

* `TensorSym K m n` — the free `K`-module on the abstract symbols, built by
  hand as an inductive (`nil`, `sym`, `add`, `smul`), because the P.0.4
  pin-run census found `Finsupp.single` CHOICE-TAINTED at the pin
  (`docs/AUDITS/2026-10-30-axiom-lint-pin-run.md`), so the free module on
  symbols is *not* `Finsupp`.
* `TensorChain K m n x y` — an inductive **in `Type u`** (witness chains are
  data): the equivalence/congruence laws of the free module plus Hörmann's
  relations (i)–(iv) verbatim (`zeroSymRight`, `zeroSymLeft`, `addSymLeft`,
  `addSymRight`, `smulSym`) plus one scalar generator `smulSymLeft`
  (see review notes). `tensorRel x y := Nonempty (TensorChain … x y)`.
* `FreeTensor K m n := Quot (tensorRel K m n)` — the quotient; equality of
  formal sums is proof-relevant (a witness chain is data), and the only
  quotient axiom used is `Quot.sound`, which is in the lint's clean set.
* `tensorSym σ ω` (raw symbol), `tensorMk σ ω := ⟦σ⊗ω⟧`.
* Instances `Add`, `Zero`, `SMul K` on `FreeTensor`, via `Quot.map₂` /
  `Quot.lift`, with definitional peels `tensorAddMk`, `tensorSmulMk`,
  `tensorZeroMk`, `tensorSmulEq`.
* Peels of the relations as equalities in `FreeTensor`:
  `tensorZeroRight` / `tensorZeroLeft` (i), `tensorAdd` (ii),
  `tensorAddRight` (iii), `tensorSmul` (iv), plus the scalar forms
  `tensorSmulLeft` (`c(σ⊗ω) = (cσ)⊗ω`) and `tensorSmulRight`
  (`c(σ⊗ω) = σ⊗(cω)`).
* `tensorBil : FreeMod K m → FreeMod K n → FreeTensor K m n` (the canonical
  map `(σ,ω) ↦ σ⊗ω`) with `tensorBilAddLeft`, `tensorBilAddRight`,
  `tensorBilSmulLeft`, `tensorBilSmulRight` — it is bilinear.
* `TensorBilMap K m n W` — bilinear maps into any `K`-module `W` (explicit
  structure), with `map_zero_left` / `map_zero_right`.
* The universal property, proved from the construction (no `sorry`):
  `tensorLift β : FreeTensor K m n → W` (via `Quot.lift` of the recursion
  `tensorSymLift` on formal sums, shown to respect the relation by
  `tensorSymLiftRel`, a 22-case induction over `TensorChain`),
  `tensorLift_beta` (`tensorLift β (σ⊗ω) = β σ ω`), `tensorLift_add`,
  `tensorLift_smul`, `tensorLift_zero`, and `tensorLift_unique`
  (any additive, linear `f` agreeing with `β` on the symbols equals the
  lift).
* Smoke tests at `K = ℚ` (inhabitation, peel (ii), beta rule with the zero
  bilinear map).

## Literature

* F. Hörmann, *A note on formal periods*, arXiv:2106.03803, proof of Key
  Lemma 3.2 — **verified against the arXiv HTML**: "The tensor product
  `H_B(M)⊗_ℚ H_dR(M)∨` can be defined as the quotient of the Abelian monoid
  generated by abstract symbols `σ⊗ω` … modulo the relations
  (i) `x+σ⊗0 ∼ x+0⊗ω ∼ x`, (ii) `x+σ₁⊗ω+σ₂⊗ω ∼ x+(σ₁+σ₂)⊗ω`,
  (iii) `x+σ⊗ω₁+σ⊗ω₂ ∼ x+σ⊗(ω₁+ω₂)`, (iv) `x+σ⊗(λω) ∼ x+(λσ)⊗ω`, for all
  formal sums `x` and all `σ,σ₁,σ₂ ∈ H_B(M)`, `ω,ω₁,ω₂ ∈ H_dR(M)∨`,
  `λ ∈ ℚ*`.” The Lean constructors `zeroSymRight`, `zeroSymLeft`,
  `addSymLeft`, `addSymRight`, `smulSym` transcribe (i)–(iv) verbatim (with
  `λ : K` arbitrary; Hörmann states `λ ∈ ℚ*`, and the `λ = 0` case is covered
  by (i)). No `REVIEW(citation)` tag.
* D. Dummit & R. Foote, *Abstract Algebra*, 3rd ed., Wiley 2004, §10.4
  "Tensor Products of Modules" — the generators-and-relations construction
  of `M ⊗_R N` and its universal property (bilinear maps factor uniquely
  through `M ⊗_R N`).
* Lean 4 core, `Init/Prelude.lean` / `Init/Core.lean` (`Quot`, `Quot.sound`,
  `Quot.lift`, `Quot.ind`), and mathlib `Mathlib/Data/Quot.lean`
  (`Quot.map₂`, `Quot.map₂_mk`, `Quot.lift_mk`, `Quot.induction_on₂`).

## Where it would live in Mathlib

Not a Mathlib candidate in this form: mathlib's `TensorProduct` is built on
`Finsupp`, and `Finsupp.single` is CHOICE-TAINTED at the pin (P.0.4 census),
while this module is the program-owned constructive replacement in
`BoringMath/FormalPeriods/`, drafted upstreamable in style. If a
choice-free `TensorProduct` were ever proposed upstream, the witness-chain
presentation here is the template.

## Does Mathlib already have it?

Mathlib has `TensorProduct` (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/`), whose
`tmul` lints CLEAN at the pin but whose underlying `Finsupp.single` is
CHOICE-TAINTED — so `TensorProduct` as a whole is not lint-CLEAN and is not
used on this path (per ticket #107's scope). What Mathlib lacks is the
witness-chain (proof-relevant equality of formal sums) presentation; the
universal property `TensorProduct.lift` exists but routes through the
choice-tainted construction.

## Review notes — design decisions to referee

1. **Free module (not just monoid) on the symbols.** Hörmann quotients the
   free *abelian monoid*; the ticket prescribes the free *module* (an
   inductive with `nil/add/sym/smul`). With a `smul` constructor present,
   the verbatim relation (iv) `x+σ⊗(λω) ∼ x+(λσ)⊗ω` alone does not identify
   `λ(σ⊗ω)` with `(λσ)⊗ω` in the quotient over an arbitrary field (over a
   field of characteristic zero it is derivable from (ii) and (iv) by
   clearing denominators; in positive characteristic it is not). We
   therefore add **one extra generator** `smulSymLeft`:
   `x + c(σ⊗ω) ∼ x + (cσ)⊗ω`. In Hörmann's monoid presentation this
   relation is not a quotient relation but the *definition* of the scalar
   action on the quotient (there is no scalar multiplication on formal sums
   there), so the construction is faithful to Key Lemma 3.2; over `ℚ` the
   two presentations coincide. Relations (i)–(iv) are transcribed verbatim
   regardless.
2. **`Rat.instField` is CHOICE-TAINTED at the pin** (new pin-run finding,
   `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`). Hence every *named*
   declaration of the module is generic in `K`/`W` and lints CLEAN; only the
   unlinted `ℚ` smoke `example`s instantiate `K = ℚ` (they elaborate and
   check fine, but are not lint-graded declarations).
3. Equality of formal sums is proof-relevant as required: `TensorChain` is
   `Type u`-valued and `Quot.sound` is fed `Nonempty.intro` of a chain.
